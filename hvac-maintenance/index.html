Signs You Need HVAC Maintenance in Conway

  • New noises when the equipment starts up
  • A breakdown last season that arrived with zero warning

Why Hire a Professional for HVAC Maintenance?

Homeowners can take care of a short list: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and glancing at the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded give no visible warning. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks just like a new one. Low charge shows up only in superheat and subcooling readings. Cracked heat exchangers, weak flame signals, marginal pressure switches, and amp draws closing in on the breaker's limit are all invisible until measured, and all of them cost less to fix the season before they fail.

Gas heating adds a safety dimension that goes beyond cost. Burners, flame proving, venting, and draft should be verified with instruments every year, because when they fail, the result can be combustion gases in your living space, not just a chilly house. That is why our experienced technicians treat safety testing as the center of every fall visit rather than an add-on. Maintenance Tips for HVAC Maintenance

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Test carbon monoxide detectors monthly if any of your equipment burns fuel
  • Replace your filter on a one- to three-month cycle; nothing else you do pays off more
  • Book spring and fall visits in the shoulder seasons, before the rush and before the extremes
  • Pay attention to new noises or longer cycles and mention them at your next visit
  • Trim plants back from the outdoor unit and give the coil a gentle rinse every spring
  • Leave every supply and return register open, including those in unused rooms
  • Clear the condensate drain early in cooling season, before algae has a chance to grow
