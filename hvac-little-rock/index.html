Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Little Rock

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of summer.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
