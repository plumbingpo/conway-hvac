Where do most HVAC installations go wrong? Most often before the first wrench turns, when someone matches the new tonnage to the old nameplate and skips the math. At Conway HVAC, the math comes first. We build a load calculation for your Conway, AR home from its square footage, insulation levels, windows, orientation, and air leakage, because a system sized by habit rather than by measurement is wrong from day one. From there the job becomes three connected pieces of work: equipment matched to the load, ductwork able to deliver what that equipment produces, and a commissioning process that proves the result with real readings instead of on paper.

Once the load is known, we select equipment to fit it, and we pick it as a matched set. Manufacturers rate the outdoor unit, indoor coil, and blower together, so a mismatched pairing quietly gives up the SEER2 and AFUE figures printed on the sticker. You will get a plain-language explanation of what single-stage, two-stage, and variable-speed equipment actually do, where an ECM blower earns its cost, and whether a furnace, air handler, or heat pump makes the most sense for your house, with no pressure attached. The ducts get the same scrutiny. Our crew measure static pressure, track down leaky or undersized runs, and fix the chokepoints while everything is open, since new equipment on bad ducts never reaches its rating.

The final stretch is where a careful installer and a fast one look nothing alike. Our technicians pressure-test and evacuate the line set to a micron-verified vacuum, weigh in the charge and prove it with superheat and subcooling readings, leak-test the gas side, vent to the manufacturer's spec, and trap the condensate with float-switch protection. The thermostat gets configured for your equipment's real staging rather than left on defaults. Then comes full commissioning: temperature split, temperature rise, amp draws, and static pressure, all recorded and explained to you before we haul the old system away. Why Hire a Professional for HVAC Installation?

Installation decides how the biggest machine in your Conway home will behave for the next fifteen years, and the costly errors are hard to spot at handoff. An unverified vacuum leaves moisture to breed acid inside the refrigerant circuit. A charge set by guesswork gives away efficiency month after month. A mismatched coil and condenser can never deliver the rated SEER2. Restrictive ducts force the blower to fight excess static pressure for its entire life. Add federal refrigerant rules and the safety stakes of gas piping and venting, and this is clearly not a job for guesswork.

Our experienced technicians treat installation as a chain of proofs, not a list of tasks. The load calculation proves the size. Matched equipment makes the rating reachable. A pressure test and a micron-gauge vacuum prove the circuit is tight and dry, while a weighed charge checked against superheat and subcooling proves the refrigerant side. On the heating side, gas lines are leak-tested, draft is verified, and blower speed is set to hold the rated temperature rise. Finally, commissioning puts the numbers on paper, so every future service visit has something to check against. That documentation is standard on every system we install in Conway.

HVAC Installation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Start a regular filter schedule right away, since steady airflow protects the coil and the heat exchanger
  • Keep the outdoor unit rinsed and clear so the condenser coil never runs starved
  • Call about new noises or slipping performance early, when fixes are simple
  • Flush the drain line before summer to keep algae from clogging it
  • Keep supply and return vents open to preserve the airflow balance set at commissioning
  • Save your commissioning report and share it with whoever services the system
  • Book professional maintenance twice a year, spring for cooling and fall for heating
